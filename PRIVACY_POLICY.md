# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** June 6, 2026

Privacy for WhatsApp Web (Auto Blur WA) ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our Google Chrome extension.

## 1. Single Purpose

The sole purpose of Privacy for WhatsApp Web (Auto Blur WA) is to provide screen privacy features (such as blurring text, names, profile pictures, and media elements) on the WhatsApp Web interface to protect users from shoulder-surfing in public or professional environments.

## 2. No Data Collection (100% Local Processing)

- **No Personal Data:** We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, tracking identifiers, or analytics data.
- **No Chat Content Tracking:** The extension operates entirely within your local browser ecosystem. It cannot read, log, or export your messages, contact lists, or account details to any external servers.
- **No Telemetry:** There are no third-party tracking scripts, analytics modules, or telemetry tools embedded inside the extension software.

## 3. Data Storage & Permissions

- **Storage Permission:** Any configurations you make (such as custom pixel blur amounts, background idle timeout limits, or dark/light mode preferences) are stored locally on your device via the `chrome.storage.local` API. This data never leaves your browser.
- **Host Permissions:** The extension requests host permissions for `https://web.whatsapp.com/*` exclusively to inject the local CSS and layout scripts required to blur your UI elements on hover.

## 4. Open Source & Transparency

This extension is a multi-generational community continuation fork built on open-source software and released under the MIT License. Our full codebase is completely transparent and publicly viewable for auditing on our official repository.

## 5. Third-Party Disclaimer

This extension is an independent, community-driven open-source project and has no official relationship, affiliation, or endorsement from WhatsApp Inc. or Meta Platforms, Inc.
